## 1. Professional Legal/Regulatory Review Required

### 1.1 Payment Orchestration Model

**Review Area**: Payment orchestration model

**Description**: Review NeoWallet's payment orchestration model to determine regulatory obligations under RBI regulations, Payment and Settlement Systems Act, and other applicable frameworks.

**Questions for Legal Counsel**:
- Does NeoWallet's payment orchestration model require RBI approval?
- Does NeoWallet's payment orchestration model require payment aggregator licensing?
- What are the regulatory obligations for payment orchestration through authorized/regulated providers?
- What are the data sharing requirements with payment providers?
- What are the liability considerations for payment orchestration?
