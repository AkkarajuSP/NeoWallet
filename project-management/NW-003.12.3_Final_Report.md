# NW-003.12.3 Final Report — Unified Financial Experience

## Objective

Build a cohesive mobile financial management experience across transactions, budgets, savings goals, bills, financial health, and Neo AI contextual entry points. All work respected family context, server-side authorization, soft deletion, completed transaction immutability, and the existing financial health v1.1 / business-rule guardrails. No payment execution or money movement features were introduced.

## Completed Changes

### Neo AI Contextual Navigation

- `lib/core/routing/router.dart` — Added `/neo` route that accepts `familyId` and `initialPrompt` in `extra` and passes them to `AiChatScreen`.
- `lib/features/ai/screens/ai_chat_screen.dart` — `AiChatScreen` now accepts `familyId` and `initialPrompt`, pre-fills the input, and sends the initial message on first frame.
- `lib/core/components/neo_context_button.dart` — New `NeoContextButton` and `NeoContextChip` components for consistent, contextual Neo AI entry points.

### Family Context Switching

All list and health screens consume `selectedFamilyIdProvider` and pass `familyId` into their `.family` providers. Because the providers are family-scoped (`StateNotifierProvider.family`), switching families automatically builds a new notifier instance and reloads data — preventing stale data across screens.
