# NW-003.8.2 — Authorization Role Consistency Cleanup

## Objective
Remove the unreachable `ADMIN` family-role branch from budget and savings-goal authorization, and confirm that the approved family roles (`OWNER`, `MEMBER`, `RESTRICTED`) remain the only product family roles.

## Summary
- `ADMIN` was confirmed **not** to be a valid or storable NeoWallet family role.
- `ADMIN` was removed from `BudgetService` and `SavingsGoalService` family write guards.
- The audit-log `actor_type` `ADMIN` was **intentionally retained**; it is a separate system concept.
- The `NW-003.8_final_report.md` wording was corrected.
- New tests confirm `OWNER`, `MEMBER`, and `RESTRICTED` permissions, and that `ADMIN` cannot be invited as a family role.
- All build and test commands passed.
