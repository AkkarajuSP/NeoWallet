## 4. Compliance Notes

- **No payment execution or money movement implemented.** `mark as paid` only records `status = PAID`, `paidDate`, and `paymentMethod`.
- **No ADMIN role introduced.** Existing `OWNER`, `MEMBER`, `RESTRICTED` roles are respected:
  - `OWNER`: full control
  - `MEMBER`: create/update/list but cannot mark family bills as paid or delete family bills
  - `RESTRICTED`: cannot create or modify bills
- **Monetary values** use `BigDecimal` on the backend and `NUMERIC(15,2)` in the database.
- **Audit logging** is performed for create, update, delete, and mark-paid actions.
- **OpenAPI contract** remains valid after changes.

## 5. Known Limitations / Follow-ups

- Recurring bill expansion is not implemented; `recurringPeriod` is stored but future instances are not generated.
- Reminder notifications are planned in the business rules but not wired to the notification service in this MVP.
- The edit action on the bill detail screen navigates to the create/edit screen via `extra: billId` using the existing `/bills/create` route.
