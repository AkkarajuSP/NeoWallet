# NW-003.4 — NeoWallet Family Management Final Report

## 1. Scope

Implemented the NeoWallet Family Management vertical slice (NW-003.4) covering family creation, viewing, updating, deletion, member listing, invitations, role management, acceptance/rejection, and leaving/deleting the family. ## 4. Security Controls

- **Server-side authorization**: Every family-scoped operation re-verifies the caller's active membership and role from `family_members`.
- **Role enforcement**: Only `OWNER`, `MEMBER`, and `RESTRICTED` are permitted. Invitation roles limited to `MEMBER` and `RESTRICTED`. Role changes cannot target `OWNER`.
- **Cross-family isolation**: All queries include `family_id` and `left_at IS NULL`; members cannot access other families.
- **Privilege escalation prevention**: `RESTRICTED` members cannot invite. Non-owners cannot update family details or change member roles.
- **Invitation lifecycle**: Tokens are URL-safe, random, 24-hour expiring, single-use. Re-acceptance returns 404.
- **Audit logging**: All sensitive family operations emit `AuditLog` events.
